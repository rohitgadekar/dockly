## 🎬 PRODUCTION TIPS

### Tools You Can Use

1. **Video Editing:**
   - Adobe Premiere Pro
   - Final Cut Pro
   - DaVinci Resolve (Free)

2. **Motion Graphics:**
   - After Effects
   - Motion (Mac)
   - Canva (Simple animations)

3. **Screen Recording:**
   - OBS Studio (Free)
   - Screen Studio (Mac - Premium)
   - Loom (Quick option)

4. **AI Video Tools:**
   - Runway ML
   - Synthesia (Voiceover)
   - Descript (All-in-one)

### Shooting the Dashboard Footage

1. **Open Docked.Pro in browser**
2. **Set resolution to 1920x1080**
3. **Use Screen Studio or similar for crisp recording**
4. **Slow, smooth mouse movements**
5. **Highlight key features with zoom/focus**
6. **Record dark mode for cinematic feel**

### Voiceover

- **Professional:** Hire on Fiverr ($50-200)
- **AI:** Use ElevenLabs or Synthesia
- **DIY:** Record in quiet room with decent mic
- **Tone:** Confident, premium, aspirational
