# Quick Start: Creating Your Marketing Video

## 🚀 FASTEST METHOD (1-2 Hours)

### Use Canva Video (Recommended for Beginners)

1. **Go to Canva.com** (Pro account recommended)
2. **Search for:** "Product Demo Video" or "SaaS Launch Video"
3. **Choose a premium template** (dark/luxury themes)
4. **Customize:**
   - Replace placeholder text with script content
   - Upload screenshots of Docked.Pro dashboard
   - Change colors to match (#D4AF37 gold, #050505 black)
   - Add your logo
5. **Export:** 1080p MP4

**Time:** 1-2 hours  
**Cost:** $12.99/month (Canva Pro)  
**Quality:** Good for social media

## 💎 PROFESSIONAL METHOD (1-2 Days)

### Screen Recording + Basic Editing

#### Step 1: Record Dashboard Footage (30 mins)

**Tools Needed:**
- Screen Studio ($89) OR OBS Studio (Free)
- Browser with Docked.Pro open

**Process:**
1. Open Docked.Pro dashboard
2. Set browser to fullscreen (1920x1080)
3. Start recording
4. Slowly navigate through:
   - Identity page (5 seconds)
   - Theme switcher (3 seconds)
   - Spotify integration (5 seconds)
   - Stories section (5 seconds)
   - Analytics page (5 seconds)
   - Ventures page (5 seconds)
5. Record each section 2-3 times for options

#### Step 2: Get Voiceover (1-3 hours)

**Option A: AI (Fastest)**
- Go to ElevenLabs.io
- Paste script
- Choose "Professional Male" or "Professional Female"
- Download MP3
- **Cost:** $5-30/month
- **Time:** 10 minutes

**Option B: Hire on Fiverr**
- Search "professional voiceover"
- Send script
- Choose voice you like
- **Cost:** $50-200
- **Time:** 24-48 hours

**Option C: DIY**
- Use your phone
- Record in quiet room
- Read script slowly and clearly
- Use free tool like Audacity to clean up
- **Cost:** Free
- **Time:** 1-2 hours

#### Step 3: Edit Video (2-4 hours)

**Tool:** DaVinci Resolve (FREE) or iMovie (Mac, FREE)

**DaVinci Resolve Tutorial:**
1. **Import:**
   - Screen recordings
   - Voiceover audio
   - Background music (from Epidemic Sound/Artlist)

2. **Timeline:**
   - Drag clips to match script timing
   - Cut/trim to voiceover
   - Add transitions (cross dissolve 0.5s)

3. **Add Text:**
   - Title card: "DOCKED.PRO"
   - Feature highlights from script
   - Call-to-action: "Get Started at docked.pro"
   - Use Syncopate or similar bold font

4. **Color Grade:**
   - Increase overall brightness by 10%
   - Boost gold/yellow tones
   - Slight vignette for cinematic feel

5. **Export:**
   - 1080p, H.264, 60fps
   - Bitrate: 10-15 Mbps

## 🤖 AI-POWERED METHOD (30 Minutes - 2 Hours)

### Using AI Tools (Cutting Edge)

#### Option A: Runway ML
1. Sign up at runwayml.com
2. Use "Gen-2" text-to-video for intro/outro
   - Prompt: "Cinematic luxury tech product reveal, golden lighting, black background, premium aesthetic"
3. Upload screen recordings for middle section
4. Auto-generate transitions
5. Add AI voiceover
6. Export

**Cost:** $12-28/month  
**Quality:** Very good  
**Time:** 1-2 hours

#### Option B: Descript (All-in-One)
1. Sign up at descript.com
2. Upload screen recordings
3. Paste script - it auto-generates voiceover
4. Edit like a document (cut words = cut video)
5. Add templates and effects
6. Export

**Cost:** $12-24/month  
**Quality:** Excellent  
**Time:** 30 min - 1 hour
