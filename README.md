## 🚀 Core Features

### ⚡ 1. The Command Deck (Mission Control)
The operational core of your digital estate.
* **Real-Time Telemetry**: Live engagement velocity, profile view analytics, click metrics, and node diagnostics.
* **Actionable Onboarding**: Dynamic estate completeness tracker guiding setup across all modules.
* **Instant vCard & QR Broadcast**: Generate custom-styled QR codes and downloadable `.vcf` digital business cards with a single click.

### 🛡️ 2. Sovereign Dossier (CV & Resume Vault)
Protect and deliver your professional curriculum vitae with sovereign control.
* **Passcode Protection**: Gate your resume or confidential portfolio behind custom access codes.
* **Pixel-Perfect PDF Generation**: One-click download of your formatted dossier using client-side rendering engines.
* **Granular Visibility**: Share sensitive work history and compensation benchmarks strictly with authorized parties.

### 💼 3. Ventures & Portfolio Matrix
Showcase your products, startups, and open-source contributions.
* **Interactive Project Cards**: Display live URLs, repository links, project descriptions, and associated tech stacks.
* **Visual Status Hierarchy**: Kinetic badges indicating venture velocity (Live, In Development, Acquired, Archived).

### 🧠 4. Skills & Technical Arsenal
Organize and display your competencies with high visual fidelity.
* **Categorized Taxonomy**: Group skills into Frontend, Backend, Cloud & DevOps, AI/ML, Design, and Leadership.
* **Years of Experience & Fluency**: Transparent metrics reflecting your technical depth.
* **Brand Iconography**: Automatic brand icon mapping for 100+ modern tools and programming frameworks.

### 🧭 5. Career Milestones & Timeline
A cinematic chronicle of your professional trajectory.
* **Chronological Milestones**: Highlight pivotal promotions, founder exits, keynote talks, and funding rounds.
* **Tagging & Media Linking**: Attach external validation links, badges, and organization references to every milestone.

### ⭐ 6. Endorsements & Social Proof
Build instant credibility with verified client and peer testimonials.
* **Client Vault**: Manage references from founders, executive leaders, and clients.
* **Verified Rating Matrix**: Display 5-star ratings, quotes, company badges, and reviewer titles.

### 🎵 7. Sonic Identity (Spotify Integration)
Infuse your digital estate with your auditory personality.
* **My Anthem**: Spotlight your current personal theme track directly on your profile.
* **Top 5 Matrix**: Curate your definitive 5-track auditory signature.
* **Live Spotify Sync**: Fetches real-time metadata, artwork, and 30-second audio preview snippets.

### 🛍️ 8. Marketplace & Booking Inquiries
Monetize your expertise and curate recommended products.
* **Curated Products**: Showcase physical products, books, digital tools, hardware setups, or affiliate recommendations.
* **Client Inquiries & Booking Modal**: Frictionless inquiry and booking modal enabling prospective clients to initiate contact with tailored budget ranges and project scopes.

### 🎨 9. Atmospherics (Live Visual Engine)
Full creative sovereignty over how your public profile renders across devices.
* **Curated Haute Themes**: Instantly toggle between signature palettes:
  * **Obsidian Royale** (Deep black with 24k gold accents)
  * **Monaco Rosso** (Carbon fiber with racing crimson)
  * **Tokyo Cyberpunk** (Midnight OLED with neon cyan)
  * **Terminal 01** (Phosphor cathode green on pitch black)
  * **Glacial Slate** (Minimalist editorial slate)
* **Architectural Typography**: Switch typography pairings across Syncopate, Space Grotesk, JetBrains Mono, Bodoni Moda, Cinzel, and Outfit.
* **Responsive Neural Preview**: Real-time simulated preview for desktop, tablet, and mobile viewpoints before broadcasting changes.

### 📊 10. Privacy-First Analytics (Audience Telemetry)
* **Cookieless Tracking**: Measure engagement and link performance without invasive trackers or GDPR compliance friction.
* **Detailed Breakdown**: Monitor referrer origins, browser platforms, device distributions, and geographic telemetry.

## 🎨 Design System: "Maison Archive"

Docked.pro is designed around the principles of digital luxury, tactile responsiveness, and structural minimalism:

* **Depth & Materials**: Multi-layered glassmorphic panels (`backdrop-blur-md`), subtle noise overlays, and fine-line borders (`border-white/10`).
* **Kinetic Micro-Interactions**: Magnetic hover actions, gentle scale dampening (powered by `framer-motion`), and buttery smooth inertial scrolling with `Lenis`.
* **Visual Hierarchy**: High-contrast typographic scale paired with soft ambient glows and muted gold/crimson/emerald accents.

## 🛠 Technology Stack

### Frontend & Core
* **Framework**: [React 19](https://react.dev/) + [TypeScript](https://www.typescriptlang.org/)
* **Build Engine**: [Vite 7](https://vitejs.dev/) with `@vitejs/plugin-react-swc`
* **Routing**: [React Router v7](https://reactrouter.com/)
* **State & Data Fetching**: [TanStack Query v5](https://tanstack.com/query/latest)

### Styling & Motion
* **CSS Framework**: [TailwindCSS v3](https://tailwindcss.com/) + `tailwindcss-animate`
* **Animations**: [Framer Motion v12](https://www.framer.com/motion/)
* **Smooth Scrolling**: [Lenis](https://lenis.darkroom.engineering/) + `@studio-freight/react-lenis`
* **Icons**: [Lucide React](https://lucide.dev/) + [React Icons](https://react-icons.github.io/react-icons/)
* **Charts & Data Viz**: [Recharts](https://recharts.org/)

### Backend & Security
* **BaaS**: [Supabase](https://supabase.com/)
  * **Database**: PostgreSQL with Row-Level Security (RLS)
  * **Authentication**: Email/Password, Magic Link, and Secure Session Vault
  * **Storage**: Avatar and digital asset bucket storage
* **Security & Forms**: [Zod](https://zod.dev/) + [React Hook Form](https://react-hook-form.com/) + [Crypto-JS](https://cryptojs.altervista.org/)
* **Export Utilities**: `html-to-image`, `qrcode.react`, `qr-code-styling`

## ⚡ Quick Start

### 1. Prerequisites
* **Node.js**: `v18.0.0` or higher
* **Package Manager**: [pnpm](https://pnpm.io/) (recommended), `npm`, or `yarn`
* A free [Supabase](https://supabase.com/) account

### 2. Clone Repository
```bash
git clone https://github.com/rohitgadekar/docked.pro.git
cd docked.pro
```

### 3. Install Dependencies
```bash
pnpm install
# or
npm install
```

### 4. Configure Environment Variables
Copy the example environment file and supply your Supabase credentials:

```bash
cp .env.example .env
```

Edit `.env`:
```env
# Supabase Configuration
VITE_SUPABASE_URL=https://your-project-id.supabase.co
VITE_SUPABASE_KEY=your-supabase-anon-key

# Optional Integrations
VITE_YOUTUBE_API_KEY=your-youtube-api-key
```

### 5. Setup Database
Execute the database schema in your Supabase SQL Editor. See [Database & Supabase Setup](#-database--supabase-setup) below.

### 6. Start Development Server
```bash
pnpm dev
# or
npm run dev
```

Visit `http://localhost:5173` to explore the app.

## 🗄️ Database & Supabase Setup

Docked.pro is powered by PostgreSQL hosted on Supabase with strict **Row-Level Security (RLS)** to enforce privacy boundaries.

### Initializing Tables & Policies

1. Navigate to your **Supabase Dashboard** > **SQL Editor**.
2. Open [`supabase_schema.sql`](file:///Users/rohit/Downloads/docked.pro/supabase_schema.sql) in this repository and execute the script.
3. This sets up the following core schema:
   * **`profiles`**: User metadata, theme customization, top tracks, and privacy controls.
   * **`ventures`**: Portfolio projects, tech stacks, and live links.
   * **`skills`**: Technical competencies, categories, and experience levels.
   * **`milestones`**: Career timeline events and achievements.
   * **`endorsements`**: Verified client reviews and testimonials.
   * **`curated_products`**: Products, affiliate links, and commercial goods.
   * **`social_links`**: Social platforms and custom contact routes.
   * **`inquiries`**: Direct client booking and inbound correspondence.
   * **`profile_views` & `link_clicks`**: Privacy-compliant telemetry data points.
   * **`newsletter_subscribers`**: Audience email capture.

### Storage Bucket Setup
1. In the Supabase Dashboard, create a public storage bucket named **`avatars`**.
2. Enable public read access so profile images and venture screenshots render correctly.

## 🚢 Deployment

### Deploying to Vercel
The repository includes a pre-configured [`vercel.json`](file:///Users/rohit/Downloads/docked.pro/vercel.json) supporting Single Page Application (SPA) client-side routing.

1. Push your repository to GitHub.
2. Import the repository in [Vercel](https://vercel.com).
3. Set the build framework preset to **Vite**.
4. Configure the environment variables (`VITE_SUPABASE_URL`, `VITE_SUPABASE_KEY`).
5. Deploy.

### Production Build
To create a minified, production-ready build locally:
```bash
pnpm build
pnpm preview
```

The output artifacts will be generated in `/dist`.
